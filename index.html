Welcome to Taniti!

The Taniti Basics

Taniti is a small, tropical island in the Pacific. While the island has an area of less than 500 square miles, the terrain is varied and includes both sandy and rocky beaches, a small but safe harbor, lush tropical rainforests, and a mountainous interior that includes a small, active volcano. Taniti has an indigenous population of about 20,000. Until a recent increase in tourism, most the Tanitian economy was dominated by fishing or agriculture.

First Thing's First...

Restaurants

Photo by Snapwire on StockSnap

Taniti currently has 10 restaurants: five serve mostly local fish and rice, three serve American-style meals, and two serve Pan-Asian cuisine. Six are located along the coastline of the island, while the others are at different areas inland. All restaurants are graded according to the standards of the Tanitian Department of Health. Four of the island's restaurants are rated at four stars or above, so expect the best cuisine in Taniti!

Lodging

Taniti has a wide variety of lodging that ranges from an inexpensive hostel to one large, four-star resort. There are many small, family-owned hotels and a growing number of bed and breakfasts. All types of lodging are strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government. Some visitors to the island participate in ownership by purchasing time-share condos. These are beautiful and luxurious homes on or near the beach. Repeat visitors love our condos and our bed and breakfasts. Come and stay in Taniti, there's a place for everyone!

Grocery Stores

Photo by Lukas Budimaier on StockSnap

Taniti has two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores, and one convenience store that is open 24 hours a day. The supermarkets are exactly what you would expect from any supermarket, but also with food produced from local vegetation, including exotic fruits, such as the seahorse fruit, found only on Taniti. Seahorse fruit has an iridescent teal color, and is tangy and sweet. Our 24-hour convenience store is much larger than it sounds, providing for any common needs at any hour. Items are kept well stocked by frequent deliveries by ship and plane, as well as many items from local manufacturing, such as clothing, brushes, and shoes.
