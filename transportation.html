Taniti Transportation

Air Transportation

Almost all visitors arrive to Taniti by air, though some arrive on a small cruise ship that docks in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night per week. Taniti is served by a small airport that can accommodate small jets and propeller planes. Taniti is in the process of expanding the airport so larger jets will be able to land on the island within the next few years.

Intra-island Public Transportation

Photo by Humphrey Muleba on StockSnap

Public buses serve Taniti City and run from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day. Private buses serve the rest of the island. Taxis are available in Taniti City, as well as ride sharing with locals and other visitors to the island. The Tanitian Government just upgraded all the island's buses, allowing for a more modern and comfortable ride with much storage for personal items and luggage. They are air-conditioned and are easily identifiable by the yellow leaves all over the buses, representing the island's Yellow Leaf Bay.

Foot Power

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable. Many tourists stay in the area surrounding Merriton Landing: this area is easy to explore on foot. Many visitors enjoy strolling along Taniti Citiy's many avenues. There are many local businesses to explore while on foot. The crime rate in Taniti City is very low, amongst the lowest of international cities. The Tanitian Government has low tolerance for an crimes, especially those that would deter visitors from the Island. Additionally, by the indigenous and local population, it is considered highly dishonorable to break Tanitian law, and criminals are dealt with swiftly. Safety of visitors and locals is of the upmost priority to the people of Taniti.

Bike Riding

Photo by Irene Lasus on StockSnap

Bikes and helmets are available to rent from several vendors (helmets are required by law). Other businesses also bike helmets with handmade designs by the locals. Biking is allowed in Taniti City, as well as through specific bike trails into the rainforest. These trails have many beautiful stops along them for a quick rest or a longer picnic. The weather is nearly always between 60 and 85 degrees Fahrenheit, ideal for virtually any activity.
