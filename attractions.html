Taniti Attractions

Tanitian Rainforest

The one thing every visitor notices on their approach to the island of Taniti, regardless of transportation method, is Taniti's beautiful rainforest. The Tanitian rainforest surrounds nearly every locale on the island, and so visitors may feel and enjoy its exquisite beauty. The Tanitian Rainforest, called Odo'jibwe by the indigenous population, boasts gorgeous flowers in hues found only in the tropics, as well as giant trees called Jigaantos by the locals, which can grow as high as a 5-story building. Additionally, due to Taniti's isolated location in the Pacific Ocean, the island is home to 4 dozen species found nowhere else on the planet. These beautiful sights offered by the Taniti rainforest can only be properly absorbed by being a visitor to the island, so come see us and enjoy the exotic beauty that is Odo'jibwe!

Taniti's Active Volcano

Photo by Skitter Photo on StockSnap

Another unforgettable experience on the island of Taniti is to hike the mountain with a local Tanitian tour guide. With our local guides, the experience will be memorable, as they can show visitors the various features of the volcanic mountain, rising up in the middle of the island's center. The island itself, especially locations along the foot of the volcano, are full of historical markers, sacred to the indigenous population on Taniti, who call the mountain Mat'a'mele, or Sleeping Fury. Although some of these specific locations are sacred to the local population, they are no secret. In fact, they wish to share their history and stories with all who visit the island of Taniti. Please come and visit Taniti, and take pictures, so that you can display proof that you have climbed the slopes of Mat'a'mele, the island's Sleeping Fury.

Helicopter Rides

Most visitors to Taniti spend most of their time in Taniti City, which boasts native architecture and nearby white, sandy beaches that encircle Yellow Leaf Bay. This natural blend of the nature of Taniti merging into the semi-urban city of Taniti City is a wonderful site to see. Want to see if from above? Well, Taniti offers daily helicopter rides around the entirety of Taniti city, including its glittering beaches of white sand found all around the entire Yellow Leaf Bay. These exciting rides run every 3 hours from 9am to 6pm, local time. The pilots are the best trained in the industry, and know how to keep their passengers safe, while still offering the thrill of a Taniti City flyby. Visit us today; your helicopter flight awaits!.

Other Attractions

Photo by Ian Livesey on StockSnap

Most people visit Taniti to enjoy the beaches, explore the rainforest, and to visit the volcano. However, there are other things to do, including visiting a local history museum, going on chartered fishing tours, snorkeling, zip-lining in the rainforest, visiting several pubs, including a microbrewery, dancing at a new dance club, seeing a movie, taking helicopter rides, playing at an arcade, visiting art galleries, and bowling. The list goes on and on, making Taniti a destination for anyone's interests. Also, a nine-hole golf course should be operational by next year, and as more people visit the island, the more venues and activities Taniti can provide as time goes on. Many of these activities are located in Merriton Landing, which is a rapidly developing area on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay.
